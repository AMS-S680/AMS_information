Surface Science and Thin Film Technology 表面科學和薄膜技術
 
This course introduces surface science, the fabrication, characterization, and physical properties of thin films. The topics include vacuum science and technology, basic 
concepts of surface science, thin film deposition techniques, kinetics and thermodynamics of surface reactions, film growth processes and modes, characterization, epitaxy, 
and physical properties of thin films. State-of-the-art research advancements on thin film preparation and properties are also selectively introduced.
本科主要介紹表面科學、薄膜加工製備表徵方法和相關的物理性質，內容包括：真空知識和技術、表面科學基礎知識、各種鍍膜技術、薄膜生長過程機理、薄膜基本特性的測
量、外延生長、半導體複雜結構合成工程、以及薄膜的光學和電學性質。本科也將有選擇地介紹當前表面科學和薄膜科研的前沿進展。

COURSE OUTCOMES
Learning Outcomes:
(1) To learn the basic principles of surface science.
(2) To know the technologies that are related to thin films.
(3) To understand the basic principles of vacuum science and technology that are related to surface science and thin-film 
deposition techniques.
(4) To understand the thermodynamics of evaporation and learn the basic principles of evaporation.
(5) To acquire the basic knowledge about adsorption, surface diffusion, nucleation, and structure development that are involved 
in thin-film deposition.
(6) To acquire the basic knowledge of epitaxial deposition.
(7) To learn the basic knowledge of chemical vapor deposition.
(8) To learn the basic techniques of measuring the physical and chemical properties of thin films.
 
Course Syllabus:
1: Introduction to Surface Science and Thin Film Technology
2: Vacuum Science and Technology
3: Surface Science
4: Evaporation
5: Deposition
6: Epitaxy
7: Chemical Vapor Deposition
8: Analysis and Properties of Thin Films
