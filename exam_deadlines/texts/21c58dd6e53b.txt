Some characteristics of this course
An experimental course
Emphasize how to apply learned knowledge to problem solving
Lack coherency among the contents
Contain many sub-topics, which are not closely related
Many qualitative descriptions and estimations, without 
rigorous derivations
Mainly based on lectures and notes
One experimental demonstration, but without assessment

Notes (Continued)
An experimental course. Useful for those who work on thin films and 
solid materials, and those who are interested in knowing more about 
thin films and solid materials.
I highly suggest you work and submit homework assignments and 
give oral presentations. Their points are easy to get. The points for 
the two exams are not easy.
The exams will consist of multiple-choice questions and long 
questions. The former are taken from the lecture slides, and the latter 
are modified from questions in the homework assignments.
