imperfections (or defects) are always present

perfection

▣Point defects (0D)
▣Linear defects (1D)
▣Planar defects/interfacial defects/boundaries (2D)
▣Bulk/volume defects (3D)

▣Impurity or foreign atoms are always present in metals
▣Current refining techniques can hardly achieve purity > 99.9999%
▣Most familiar metal materials are alloys, in which impurity atoms were 
intentionally added to impart specific characteristics, such as mechanical 
strength, corrosion resistance, etc.
▣Depending on the kind of impurity, the concentration of impurity, and the 
temperature of the alloy, addition of impurity atoms could result in formation of 
a solid solution and/or a new second phase

=== PAGE 6 ===
▣A solid solution forms when the crystal structure is maintained and no new 
structures are formed; the impurity atoms are randomly and uniformly 
dispersed with in the solid 
▣In substitutional solid solution, impurity atoms replace the host atoms
▣In interstitial solid solution, impurity atoms fill the voids among the host atoms
